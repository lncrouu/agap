# AGAP User Guide

## For residents and visitors

### Start using AGAP
1. Choose whether you are in Batangas or using guest access.
2. Open the dashboard. You can use all four features below as a guest.

### 1. Submit a hazard report
1. Select **Submit Hazard**.
2. Enter the hazard title, severity, location, and a short description. You can use the map to set the location.
3. Add at least one clear evidence photo.
4. Submit the report and save the ticket number.

### 2. Track a report
1. Select **Report Tracker** on the dashboard.
2. Review the report status and timeline for your device.
3. Select **Refresh Status** to check for updates. Keep your ticket number for reference.

### 3. Find and call a hotline
1. Select **Hotline Directory**.
2. Search by department or choose a category such as police, fire, or medical.
3. Select **Show Suggested Departments Near My Location** to see nearby options. Allow location access for a more accurate list.
4. Select a hotline's call button to open your phone dialer.

For an emergency that needs immediate help, call **911** or a local hotline shown in the app.

### 4. Check the hazard map
1. Select **Danger Zone Map**.
2. Allow location access to see your position and distances to hazards.
3. Choose **ALL**, **CRITICAL**, **SEVERE**, **MODERATE**, or **LOW** to filter the hazard list.
4. Select a red map pin or hazard card to see its location, details, and any available notice or photo.

The map shows reports that have moved beyond initial submission. A newly submitted report may not appear until it is reviewed.

### Request resident verification
1. Open your profile.
2. Enter your legal name, phone number, barangay, and a clear photo of your ID or residency proof.
3. Submit the request and wait for an administrator to review it.

Verification is optional. If a request is rejected, read the review note. You can submit again after 24 hours. You can change your username once every 7 days.

### If your device is penalized or banned
- A penalty pauses report submissions until it ends or an administrator lifts it. Other app features remain available.
- A ban locks the device. If you believe the ban is wrong, submit an appeal with 30 to 2,000 characters and wait for the review.
- An appeal does not unlock the device while it is pending.

## For administrators

Use the administrator access provided by your organization. Do not share it.

1. Open the profile screen and select the administrator lock.
2. Open the command portal.
3. Review submitted reports and resident verification requests.
4. For a report, review its details and evidence. Assign a response team with clear dispatch instructions when field checking is needed.
5. Review the responder's findings. Accept confirmed reports into operations, update their status, and add a notice or evidence photo when needed.
6. To reject a verification request, enter a clear reason. To penalize or ban a device, enter the reason and confirm the action.
7. Review pending ban appeals. Approve an appeal only when lifting the ban; denying an appeal keeps the ban active.
8. Use report restore or permanent delete carefully. Permanent deletion cannot be undone.

When resolving a report, attach the required proof photo. Check the ticket and target user before saving any change.

## For responders

1. Open **Profile**, then select **Responder**.
2. Wait for the administrator to dispatch a report to a response team.
3. Select the dispatched report and acknowledge it as **En Route**.
4. When you arrive, mark it **On Scene**.
5. Submit the field verdict with clear findings and a photo of the reported site.
6. Wait for the administrator to review the verdict. Once the report is in progress, update it as work proceeds.
7. To mark work complete, attach a resolution photo and submit **Resolved**.

If a report is not assigned or the status does not allow an action, contact the administrator.
